# Roadmap and KPIs

| Phase | Timeline | Scope | Exit criteria |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0. Foundations | Months 0–3 | Control Tower landing zone, network account, DX and VPN, gateway, AgentCore Observability, agent lake, triage SLM | All model traffic via gateway; end-to-end traces live |
| 1. Assist | Months 3–6 | Incident and change agents at L0–L1; Step Functions approvals; golden eval sets | Diagnosis accepted in ≥ 70% of P2–P4 |
| 2. Act | Months 6–12 | Top 25 SSM runbooks at L2–L3; service request and FinOps agents; enclave LLM live | 30% of P3–P4 resolved without human action |
| 3. Autonomous | Months 12–18 | L4 for proven actions; SecOps agent; business ops domains | MTTR down 50% |
| 4. Self-improving | 18 months + | Continuous distillation, agent-proposed runbooks and policies | Cost per resolved incident down 40% year on year |
