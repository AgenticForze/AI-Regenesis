# Learning Paths & Personas

All nineteen phases live on the same site, but almost nobody should read them front-to-back.
Pick the path that matches where you're starting from — each phase's index page also states its
prerequisites explicitly, so if you arrive mid-curriculum you know what to backfill.

## Foundations Track — new to AI

**Phase 0 → 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5**, then a choice of RAG (Phase 6) or straight to Agent
Foundations (Phase 8).

Best for: someone who needs to understand AI/ML concepts from the ground up before touching
anything agentic — a manager, analyst, or engineer moving into AI for the first time.

## Practitioner / Builder Track — some ML/GenAI experience, wants to build agents

**Phase 5 → 6 → 7 → 8 → 9 (framework landscape) → 10 → 11 → 12**, with capstones from Phase 18.

This flow is uninterrupted: Phase 13 (Build-Your-Own-LLM/SLM) sits outside the main line as an
optional detour, not a required stop — most practitioners fine-tune or use RAG rather than
pretrain a model from scratch.

Best for: someone who already has prompting/RAG/fine-tuning familiarity and wants to go straight
at agent design, frameworks, and multi-agent systems.

## Advanced / Architect Track — already building agentic systems

**Phase 10 → 11 → 12 → 13 (optional infra track) → 14 (hyperscaler foundations) → 15 (enterprise
governance) → 16 (domain deep dives) → 17 (frontier topics)**.

Best for: someone designing enterprise-grade or frontier agentic systems who needs platform
selection, governance/compliance depth, and domain-specific (Finance/Health/Telco) patterns.
